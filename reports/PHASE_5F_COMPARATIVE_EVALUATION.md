## Scientific status

The wiring factor is complete versus Bernoulli(0.5) cue -> action wiring inside a synthetic graph of tens of nodes. It is not the BANC connectome and no connectome edge was read, modified or simulated. Reward is an external scalar in task units. A weight change is the output of the declared three-factor rule and is not evidence of biological synaptic plasticity, dopamine release or learning in a fly.

## Protocol

- Core comparison: 10 independent seeds per condition, 160 training episodes, 40 held-out episodes at checkpoints [0, 160].
- Robustness: 4 seeds per cell, 120 training episodes, 24 held-out episodes at checkpoints [0, 120].
- Training and evaluation seeds come from separate SeedSequence streams declared before the run; disjointness is checked and reported.
- Evaluation uses eta = 0 on the current learned state with traces disabled, on the same held-out episodes at every checkpoint.
- The unit of replication is a seed. No statistic pools timesteps or episodes across seeds.
- Declared paired contrasts use the same seed index in both conditions. The family is Holm-Bonferroni corrected at alpha = 0.05.

## Limitations

- Success is a conditioned response of a designated action population, not competitive action selection; the selective rate stays near or below the n_cues chance level.
- Random wiring has fewer edges and lower total cue input than complete wiring, so the wiring contrast confounds topology with input strength.
- The network is small (tens of nodes); no result here tests the BANC connectome or a whole-brain simulation.
- Robustness cells are descriptive. They were not corrected for multiple comparisons and are not significance claims.
- The unit of replication is a seed; episodes within a seed share one weight trajectory and are not independent samples.
- The perceptron sees the correct action label and only bounds what a supervised method can do on this task.
- Evaluation uses the same held-out episodes at every checkpoint and does not test unseen cue mappings.
- Reward is delivered after a declared delay; no biological dopamine, octopamine or serotonin dynamics were simulated.
